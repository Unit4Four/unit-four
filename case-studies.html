Case Study 001

The Royal View

Luxury Holiday Rental, Linstead, Jamaica

ClientDamian
LocationLinstead, St Catherine, Jamaica
ProjectCustom website, built from scratch
01

The Brief

Damian came to Unit Four with a newly built luxury holiday home in the hills of Linstead, St Catherine, Jamaica. The property was already listed on Airbnb but had no standalone web presence. No website, no brand, nothing to show potential guests beyond a basic listing.

He needed something that matched the quality of the property itself.

02

What We Built

A fully custom luxury website built from scratch, with no templates and no page builders. The centrepiece is a cinematic scroll film hero that plays through 181 frames of aerial and interior footage as the user scrolls, giving visitors an immersive first impression before they have even seen the gallery.

  • Custom design with a dark, gold accented luxury look
  • Cinematic scroll film hero animation, 181 frames in landscape and mobile versions
  • Full property gallery with 18 high resolution photos
  • Seasonal pricing calculator built into the booking form
  • Airbnb and Booking.com integration
  • Google Maps location embed
  • House rules, amenities and local area sections
  • Deployed live on Vercel
181Frame scroll film hero
18Gallery photos
0Templates used
03

The Result

A world class property website that stands alongside luxury rental brands globally. Guests can now discover The Royal View directly, not just through Airbnb.
